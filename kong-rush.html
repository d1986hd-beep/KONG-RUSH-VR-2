Kong Rush

4 maps · cosmetics · saves your orbs on this device

Chill orb-hunting with real Gorilla Tag movement. Push off trees, rocks and the ground to run, climb and jump. Something with red eyes is out there — if it catches you it just shoves you away, no penalty.

Controls: no buttons. Swing your arms down and back against a surface, then let go to launch. Collect glowing orbs. Touch the purple ring to switch maps. In the City, spend orbs on hats, shades, fur dye and capes, then check yourself out in the mirror. Headphones recommended on the dark maps.
